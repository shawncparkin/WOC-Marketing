3. What the demonstration does — and does not — establish

Calculates: spend divided by platform conversions, and spend divided by new-customer counts supplied by the user. It handles missing denominators and invalid values.

Does not calculate: verified real-world CAC independently. It cannot confirm whether the manually entered customers are first-time buyers, deduplicate real CRM records, prove campaign attribution, or infer incremental return on ad spend.
