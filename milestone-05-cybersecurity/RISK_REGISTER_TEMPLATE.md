# Marketing data integration — risk-register working template

This is a **planning aid, not the team's completed Milestone 5 risk register**. Ella should replace the placeholder ratings with the actual risk assessment and cite supporting evidence. The presentation should show at least three risks with likelihood, impact, ownership and controls.

| Risk and WOC-specific consequence | Likelihood | Impact | Practical control | Owner / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer identity joins expose personal data or unnecessarily broaden access | To assess | To assess | Least-privilege access, MFA, data minimization, restricted exports, permission reviews | To confirm |
| Duplicate/mismatched customers make CAC inaccurate and distort budget choices | To assess | To assess | Deduplication rules, identity-match QA, source reconciliation, exception queues and metric definitions | To confirm |
| AI-generated anomaly explanations or recommendations are misleading | To assess | To assess | Human approval, sample-based evaluation, audit logs and escalation thresholds | To confirm |
| External advertising/CRM service outage or vendor change interrupts reporting | To assess | To assess | Export backups, refresh monitoring, contractual access review and documented manual fallback | To confirm |

Use likelihood/impact scales defined by the team (for example Low/Medium/High) and document why each rating was selected. Do not list a control as implemented unless it was actually implemented.
