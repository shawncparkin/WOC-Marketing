The Marketing Challenge

Making campaign spending easier to understand.

WOC relies on separate marketing-platform reports. Those conversions cannot automatically be treated as first-time customers, making true customer acquisition cost difficult to measure.

01

Disconnected reports

Google, Instagram, and email data use different sources and measurement assumptions.

02

Conversions are not customers

Cost per reported conversion is not verified new-customer acquisition cost.

03

Better decisions

Linking campaign, customer, and sales data helps WOC decide what to improve and test.

Our recommendation

Pilot a low-cost, governed reporting process that validates customer data and makes channel performance understandable.
