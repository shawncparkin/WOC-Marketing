The Business Challenge

Growth created opportunity. Fragmented systems created risk.

Wasatch Outfitters Collective has a loyal customer base and a strong regional brand. However, its rapid growth has outpaced its internal technology, financial systems, and business processes.

01

Operations

Disconnected rental and repair spreadsheets cause double-bookings and unavailable equipment.

02

Customer Support

Questions spread across phone, email, social media, and stores result in inconsistent answers.

03

Accounting

Manual reconciliation leaves nearly 30% of expenses unmatched because receipts are missing.

04

Finance

Capital purchases lack a standardized method for measuring location-specific return on investment.

05

Supply Chain

Late shipments are often discovered only after a store reports empty shelves.

06

Marketing

Separate advertising platforms make the true customer acquisition cost difficult to calculate.

07

Cybersecurity

Informal employee offboarding leaves some former seasonal workers with active system access.

Consulting Tracks

Seven perspectives. One connected business.

Each team selects one consulting track and develops a practical recommendation for WOC.

TRACK A

Digital Transformation

Redesign fragmented workflows and select a practical digital solution to improve operations.

TRACK B

AI and Analytics

Use dashboards, customer data, and responsible AI to improve decisions and customer support.

TRACK C

Cyber and Risk

Reduce cybersecurity, privacy, AI, and vendor risks while continuing to modernize.

TRACK D

Accounting

Automate and secure WOC's fragmented expense reconciliation process.

TRACK E

Finance

Use data to evaluate capital investments and forecast location-specific ROI.

TRACK F

Supply Chain

Digitize vendor management to reduce lead times and prevent inventory stockouts.

TRACK G

Marketing

Integrate digital marketing data to calculate the true customer acquisition cost.
